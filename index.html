FAQ

Common Questions

How is this different from BOSSGRAM Academy?

BOSSGRAM was a self-paced course for beginners. The Mastermind is a high-touch, small-group coaching intensive for coaches who already have an audience and want a pipeline that converts. Completely different format, depth, and level of support.

How big is each cohort?

Max 20 people per cohort. The small group size is intentional. Every person gets individual attention on their content, offer, and pipeline during the weekly calls.

Do I need a large following to join?

No. You need at least 1,000 followers and a clear expertise. The pipeline works regardless of audience size. Sarah signed 8 clients with 8,000 followers.

What if I do not have an offer yet?

We build or refine your offer in week one. But you need to know your expertise going in. If you have not decided what you want to coach on, this is not the right program yet.

What is the time commitment?

One weekly group call (90 minutes) plus implementation time (3-5 hours per week). Most of the work is building your pipeline assets, not consuming content.
